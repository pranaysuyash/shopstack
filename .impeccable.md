## Design Context

### Users
ShopStack is for households — families, roommates, and individuals — who want to stop wasting food, stop over-buying staples, and make calmer shopping decisions. Primary context is the kitchen, the grocery store, and brief daily check-ins on a phone. Users are not technical operators; they are people managing a home.

### Brand Personality
- **Warm**: feels like a helpful household member, not a dashboard.
- **Decisive**: gives clear next actions (buy / use soon / skip) without hesitation.
- **Trustworthy**: local-first, private, never hype.

Three-word personality: *warm, decisive, off-grid*.

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Warm Pantry Intelligence"** — warm cream/paper surfaces, olive green, terracotta, and amber. Light-first because it is used in kitchens and during the day. No generic SaaS blue/purple gradients, no dark-mode-by-default, no Google Fonts dependencies. System fonts keep it offline-capable. Editorial but usable; rounded cards, generous spacing, restrained motion.

Anti-references: Gradio admin panels, purple AI dashboards, generic card grids, emoji as icons, neon dark mode.

### Design Principles
1. **One action at a time.** Every screen answers "what should I do right now?"
2. **Decision badges are the anchor.** Buy / Use Soon / Skip / Compare must be instantly recognizable.
3. **Local-first surfaces.** No external fonts or cloud-required dependencies in core UI.
4. **Empty states teach.** Never just say "nothing here"; explain why and give the next step.
5. **Delight without noise.** A small celebration on completion, friendly copy, subtle motion — but never blocking or distracting.
6. **Mobile and web are siblings.** Same tokens, same nav model (6 primary destinations), same language.

### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum for web.
- Touch targets 44×44 px minimum on mobile.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Color is never the only signal; icons + text together.
